What this is

This site computes summary analytics from the public NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database — hail, wind, tornado, flood, and other significant weather events recorded across the United States since 1996. The data is the same archive used by insurance researchers, but presented as open, browsable summaries rather than per-property reports.

If you're researching a specific property, use the live tool at propertywx.com. This site is for understanding regional climatology and trends.
